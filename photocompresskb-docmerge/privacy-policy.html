1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Document Resizer & Merge (Package: swimminsparrow.compress.photo.kb.pdf.merge) is built around an uncompromising Local-First and Privacy-by-Design architecture.

Unlike online compression tools or web-based converters that require you to upload personal documents, ID cards, receipts, or contracts to third-party servers, Document Resizer performs all compression, resizing, rendering, and merging algorithms entirely within your device's local execution environment.

Your files remain strictly in your custody at all times.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Document or Image Content: We never collect, upload, inspect, transmit, or store any copy of your photos, documents, or PDF pages on any server.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or postal addresses.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request or access GPS location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).
  • No Background Behavioral Profiling: We do not log which files you resize or merge, their filenames, or your operational habits.

3. Local-Only Processing & EXIF Privacy

When you select a document or take a photo within the application:

  • Local Rendering & Native Engines: Compression, quality reduction, pixel scaling, multi-page concatenation, and watermarking are handled on-device using native Android graphics and PDF APIs.
  • EXIF & Geolocation Metadata Scrubbing: By default, the application scrubs sensitive EXIF metadata (including camera model, date taken, and embedded GPS coordinates) during image optimization to protect your privacy when sharing files. Users may optionally choose to retain EXIF tags via the advanced settings.
  • Ephemeral Cache: Temporary working bitmaps and render previews are stored strictly in the application's isolated sandboxed cache directory (context.cacheDir) and are automatically cleared.
Absolute Device Sovereignty: At no point during the compression, resizing, or merging pipeline is an internet connection required or utilized to process your documents. You can use all core features in Airplane Mode.

4. Device Permissions Used

The app requests the minimum set of permissions necessary to function, adhering to modern Android scoped storage principles:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used exclusively for in-app billing verification (Google Play / RevenueCat) and serving non-personalized/personalized ads (Google AdMob) in the free tier.
Storage Access Framework (SAF) Allows you to select files via Android's native system picker without granting the app broad read/write access to your entire storage drive.
CAMERA (Intent via FileProvider) Optional: Used only when you tap the camera button to capture a physical document or ID card. The photo is saved directly into the app's private cache via secure FileProvider.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Used strictly on legacy devices (Android 9 and older) to read selected files and save results to Downloads/Pictures. Not requested on Android 10+.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and offer free daily operations, the application integrates industry-standard third-party SDKs:

A. Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP)

In the free version of the application, ads (anchored adaptive banners, occasional interstitials, and opt-in rewarded video ads) are served via Google AdMob.

  • GDPR & EU User Consent: For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, the app integrates Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to collect and manage user consent in accordance with GDPR and ePrivacy directives.
  • Ad Identifiers: Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic performance metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Rewarded Ads: Users who reach their daily free limit may voluntarily opt to watch a rewarded video ad to receive +1 bonus operation. No reward is granted if the video is skipped or closed early.
  • 100% Ad-Free for PRO: When upgrading to Document Resizer PRO, the AdMob SDK is completely deactivated: no banners, no interstitials, and no ad network requests are made.

B. RevenueCat & Google Play In-App Billing

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and verify lifetime PRO licenses through the Google Play Store.

  • RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers and receipt tokens to validate purchase status.
  • No credit card, banking, or financial billing information is ever seen or collected by the app or developer; all transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play.
  • For more details, review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. Free Tier vs. PRO Lifetime License

  • Free Tier: Includes 2 free operations per day (with options to unlock additional operations by viewing rewarded video ads). Displays an anchored banner and occasional interstitials.
  • PRO Lifetime License: A one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited daily operations, custom KB presets, custom watermarks, EXIF management, and permanently removes 100% of advertisements and ad-related network traffic.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • App Preferences: Daily compression counters and PRO status are saved locally using encrypted Android DataStore preferences.
  • No Cloud Accounts: Because there are no user accounts or centralized servers, there is no remote account to delete.
  • Full Removal: Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently wipes all local cache files, temporary previews, and DataStore settings from your device.
